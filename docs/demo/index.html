Muse Code's reasoning, from its recorded log
Root cause: the ground lives on physics layer 5 (value 16 in tileset.tres), but the player's mask was 14 (layers 2+3+4: enemies, coins, platforms), so the ground was invisible to it and it fell straight through from the first frames. Fix: in player.tscn, collision_mask = 14 → 30, adding the ground layer while preserving all existing bits.

Why Visual Grounding (SAM 3.1) Beats Traditional QA

Without computer vision, 5 of the 8 bug variants pass conventional build checks completely undetected: missing textures, player layering behind tiles, and visual sprite offsets leave the physics coordinates valid. Meta's vision models (SAM 3.1, or the open-weight Muse Glimmer checked against the engine, also 8 of 8) see what the player actually sees on screen.

Trust Architecture: "Deny-by-Absence" & Canary Probes
